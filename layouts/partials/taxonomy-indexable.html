That reasoning is preserved here — plain tag and author terms stay noindexed — with carve-outs for curated landing pages whose titles don't compete with core pages: - blog category TERM pages (/blog/category/*) — curated kinds with hand-written descriptions - case-study industry TERM pages (/case-studies/industry/*) — curated customer-story landing pages, one per vertical (data-file source of truth) - blog series pages (/blog/series/ and /blog/series/*) — curated multi-part landing pages with unique, blog-only titles.
