Organization-level [scopes](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/scopes/) control operations on the organization itself rather than on a single stack, environment, or cloud account, such as managing members, teams, and organization settings.
Each scope lists the [built-in roles](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/roles/#pulumi-defined-roles) that grant it.
