These are the [scopes](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/scopes/) you can grant on specific [environments](/docs/esc/concepts/environments/) or sets of environments.
Creating, listing, and restoring environments are organization-level operations, so their scopes are in [Organization settings](/docs/administration/reference/rbac-scopes/org-settings/).
Each scope lists the smallest [default permission set](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/permission-sets/#default-permission-sets) that grants it.
