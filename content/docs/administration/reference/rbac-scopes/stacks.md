These are the [scopes](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/scopes/) you can grant on specific [stacks](/docs/iac/concepts/stacks/) or sets of stacks.
Creating, listing, and restoring stacks are organization-level operations, so their scopes are in [Organization settings](/docs/administration/reference/rbac-scopes/org-settings/).
Each scope lists the smallest [default permission set](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/permission-sets/#default-permission-sets) that grants it.
