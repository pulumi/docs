Manage an individual agent through its configuration page. Use **Agent Settings** for organization-wide controls.

## Agents your team creates

[Change an agent's configuration](/docs/ai/agents/manage/#change-saved-configuration), including its instructions, role, and integrations. To change its model, see [Choose a model](/docs/ai/agents/configure/#choose-a-model).

## Organization settings

- [Organization AI settings](/docs/ai/concepts/neo/settings/): enable AI features and configure defaults.
- [Model providers](/docs/ai/concepts/neo/model-providers/): connect your organization's provider account and choose a default.
- [Usage limits](/docs/ai/concepts/neo/usage-limits/): set spending limits and check which charges they cover.

## Notification preferences {#personal-preferences}

Each teammate can configure [browser and audio notifications](/docs/ai/concepts/neo/settings/#notifications) in their account settings.
