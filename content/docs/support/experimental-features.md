Pulumi releases some functionality as _experimental_ to get feedback before committing to it.
Pages that document an experimental feature say so in a warning at the top.

## What "experimental" means

- **Experimental features are opt-in.** You turn each one on explicitly with a flag, a command, or an environment variable, such as `PULUMI_EXPERIMENTAL=true`.
- **They can change or be removed at any time,** without a deprecation period.
- **They aren't necessarily supported.** Don't rely on them for production workflows.

When an experimental feature proves valuable, Pulumi stabilizes it and promotes it to general availability.
Until then, its behavior can change between releases.
