---
title: Passkey support in Pulumi Cloud
date: 2026-07-13
meta_desc: Pulumi Cloud now supports passkeys, a secure, standards-based alternative to providing your password.
---

If you sign into Pulumi Cloud with an email and password, you can now sign in with a passkey as well. A passkey is a public-key credential stored on your device (your phone, laptop, hardware key, or password manager) that lets you sign in securely without having to enter a password.

![Saving a new passkey in Pulumi Cloud](/releases/changelog/images/2026-07-13-pulumi-cloud-passkeys.png)

Passkeys are available to all users of Pulumi Cloud. Read the [announcement blog post](/blog/passkey-support-in-pulumi-cloud/) or visit [your account settings](https://app.pulumi.com/account/profile) to set up your first passkey.
