Pulumi Insights is now [Pulumi Discovery](/docs/discovery-governance/concepts/discovery/). You'll see the new name throughout the Pulumi Cloud console — in the navigation, the pages themselves, and the empty states — but nothing about how the feature works has changed. It still imports and indexes the resources across your clouds so you can search, analyze, and govern everything you're running from one place.

The new name simply describes what it does more plainly: it's how you find and keep track of your infrastructure, wherever it lives.
