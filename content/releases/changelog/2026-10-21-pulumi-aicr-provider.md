The new Pulumi NVIDIA AI Cluster Runtime (AICR) provider lets you stand up pre-validated GPU training and inference clusters across clouds in just a few lines of Python. Instead of wiring together the dozens of resources a production AI cluster needs, you describe the cluster you want and let the provider assemble it.

If you're building self-hosted AI infrastructure, it's a great time to try it out.

To learn more, read the [announcement post](/blog/self-hosted-ai-infrastructure-pulumi-aicr/).
