Pulumi Cloud has a new set of [built-in roles](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/default-roles/) — Reader, Builder, and Maintainer — that map more cleanly to how teams actually work, from someone who just needs to look around to someone who owns and administers a resource. We've also added an organization-wide default role, so new members start with exactly the access you intend, and reworked the access management UI to make all of this easier to see and change.

You'll find it under your organization's access settings in the Pulumi Cloud console. To learn more, see the [role documentation](/docs/administration/concepts/rbac/default-roles/).
