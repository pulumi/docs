The Pulumi Cloud console now has a unified inbox for all of your notifications. You'll find it in the sidebar, just above the name of the active organization, and it notifies you in real time, no reload required:

![The notification center](/releases/changelog/images/2026-08-12-notification-center.png)

The new notification center currently covers Neo tasks, ESC approvals, and billing notifications, and we'll be adding more notification types, including token usage, in the weeks ahead. There's also a full-page view of your notifications that lets you manage them more easily.

You can adjust your notification preferences — including whether to be notified in the console, by email, or both — on your [Account settings page](https://app.pulumi.com/user/account/settings).
