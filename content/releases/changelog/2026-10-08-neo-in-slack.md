You can now work with Pulumi [Neo](/docs/ai/concepts/neo/) directly from Slack. Kick off a task, pick a custom agent to run it, and follow along without leaving the channel — so the infrastructure work your team asks for happens right where the conversation is already taking place.

To learn more, see the [Neo Slack integration documentation](/docs/ai/integrations/slack/).
